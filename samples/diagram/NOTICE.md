# Diagram corpus

Every file here (and `samples/sample.drawio`, `sample.dio`, `sample.vsdx`, `sample.vstx`, `sample.vssx`,
`broken.drawio`, `broken.vsdx`) is generated from scratch by `python tools/make-diagram-samples.py` — no third-party
content — and is covered by the repository's MIT licence. The embedded PNG and EMF pictures are drawn by the script.
